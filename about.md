**Node:** 588415\
**Callsign:** KJ7T\
**Location:** Grapeview, Washington\
**Hardware:** G1LRO RLNZ2 (Radio Less Node Z2), built on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W

Node 588415 is a G1LRO RLNZ2, a compact AllStarLink node made by Mark
Herbert, GU1LRO, of [G1LRO.UK](https://g1lro.uk) in Guernsey. It runs
AllStarLink 3 on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W and connects to the network over
wired Ethernet.
